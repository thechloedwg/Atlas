Beyond the paperwork

We want to capture both the formal processes and the realities of navigating them. Your contribution might include:

  • How you approached the permission process
  • How long things actually took
  • Unexpected costs or requirements
  • People or organisations that helped
  • Challenges you encountered
  • Advice you would give another researcher

You don't need to answer everything. A useful, honest account of one part of the process is valuable too.

This is the longer account that readers can open from the gallery. You can write as much or as little as you like.

Contributions will be reviewed before publication. The form is currently a design prototype and is not yet connected to the Atlas database.

About the Atlas

The Fieldwork Atlas is a shared resource documenting the processes, permissions and experiences involved in conducting research around the world.

Research requirements can be difficult to find, change over time and vary depending on the type of research being undertaken. The Atlas brings together practical information and first-hand accounts to help researchers prepare for fieldwork and better understand the realities of working in different places.

It is a collaborative project. The information grows through the experiences and contributions of researchers.
