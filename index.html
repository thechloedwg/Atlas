About this project

The Fieldwork Atlas

The Fieldwork Atlas is a collaborative resource bringing together information and experiences about conducting research around the world.

Research permissions can be difficult to navigate. Requirements may be spread across multiple institutions, processes can change, and the experience of applying does not always match the official guidance.

The Atlas brings together two kinds of knowledge: structured information about formal research protocols, and first-hand accounts from researchers who have gone through those processes.

The aim is to make research processes more transparent, accessible and easier to navigate.

This is a collaborative resource. Contributions are reviewed before publication, and personal or sensitive information should not be shared.
