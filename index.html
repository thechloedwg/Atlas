About the Atlas

The Fieldwork Atlas is a collaborative resource bringing together information and experiences about conducting research around the world.

It aims to make research processes more transparent by sharing knowledge about permissions, institutions, practical experiences and the realities of fieldwork.

The Atlas combines structured information about research requirements with first-hand accounts from people who have undertaken research. Because knowing what the rules say is not always the same as knowing what happens in practice.

The information shared here reflects individual researchers' experiences at particular points in time. Requirements and procedures can change, so visitors should always confirm current requirements with the relevant authorities.

Published contributions will appear here as they are added to the Atlas.

Contribute to the Atlas

Contributions are reviewed before publication. Only approved, anonymised information is displayed on the Atlas. Please do not include confidential information or private contact details.
